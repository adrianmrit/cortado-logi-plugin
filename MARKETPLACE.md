**Cortado** keeps your Mac awake from your MX Creative Keypad. Choose indefinitely, a duration, or an end time, with optional display wake and live countdowns. Uses macOS's built-in *caffeinate*; no extra app required.

**Note:** Cortado does not override lid-close sleep, forced sleep, or macOS power policies.
